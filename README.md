### 👋 About me

I'm a student who learns by building the hard parts myself: the queue, the lease, the retry, the recovery path. I care about what happens when things fail, not just when they work.

- 🔭 Building **Helmrun**, a platform for running AI agents reliably at scale
- 🎞️ Designing **Tessera**, a distributed video processing pipeline
- 🧠 Practising DSA every week alongside my projects
- 📝 Every big decision gets an ADR: what we chose, what we rejected, and why

#### ⚙️ Helmrun &nbsp;<sub>`in progress` · `private for now`</sub>

A distributed platform for executing AI agents: async scheduling, multi-provider LLMs, MCP/tool integration, isolated runtimes, durable run state, retries, cancellation, checkpointing and crash recovery.

**Under the hood:** PostgreSQL as the source of truth · SQS behind a transactional outbox · one Kubernetes Job per attempt · SSRF-safe HTTP tools · OpenTelemetry traces end to end

`TypeScript` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `SQS` `Kubernetes` `Redis` `OpenTelemetry`

#### 🎞️ [Tessera](https://github.com/prometheus-97/Tessera) &nbsp;<sub>`early stage`</sub>

A distributed video processing platform that takes large uploads, transcodes them into multiple quality levels, and prepares them for reliable streaming using background workers that scale out.

**Focus:** resumable uploads · job pipelines · adaptive streaming · horizontal workers

`Distributed systems` `Background jobs` `Streaming`
